## Conditional geometric argument

Let the six-coordinate AABB be the min/max of all accepted finite triangle vertices in one component. Every exact triangle is a convex combination of its three vertices, hence every triangle lies in that AABB. For a closed, bounded, consistently oriented represented surface whose negative CSG half-space is its bounded interior, every point strictly outside the AABB belongs to the unbounded exterior. Testing instead against an *expanded* AABB can only reduce the set fast-classified as exterior in exact arithmetic. Thus the fast path is a sound geometric exterior certificate for that represented solid, assuming the component's surface and half-space semantics satisfy those premises. This is a membership proof, not an end-to-end numerical parity or transport proof.

The code calls `finite(point)` before the test and leaves the original three-ray parity path unchanged within the expanded box. It leaves `distance()` and `normal_at()` source unchanged. However, `normal_at()` uses a plane tolerance plus barycentric extrapolation and an ill-conditioned Gram determinant can magnify error for thin triangles; the fixed `1e-8 × max(1, |box coordinates|)` margin has no proven universal conditioning bound. A point far outside the expanded box that previously produced a parity-seam `nullopt` now resolves exterior. The adapter may therefore change an old unresolved/exception event to a definite exterior event even though the geometric membership is correct. The six-face and transport runs establish preservation only for those sampled states.

The independent [Pro investigation](https://chatgpt.com/c/6abde084-7428-83ea-b477-101034e78cb8) sharpened the sufficient condition: if the final triangles enclose the entire boundary of a bounded fill in the query frame and infinity is exterior, the fill lies inside their vertex box. This does not require convexity or a connected interior. It does require a specified fill rule for self-intersections or nested shells. An open cube missing one face is a counterexample to applying the shortcut to arbitrary parity ray soup: from beyond the missing face, a ray can cross the opposite face once. Periodic copies require instance-aware bounds; a curved CAD patch can bulge outside its tessellation-vertex box. The selected floating-point extrema enclose affine triangles made from the same stored vertices, but the margin is not a proven bound on every legacy coincidence acceptance point. The Pro chat explicitly did not inspect this local branch; these conclusions were checked against the source here.

The [exact Step 2 audit](P00_STEP2_CERTIFICATE_20261001.md) went beyond edge counts on the transported payload: all 18 components are closed, embedded, outward oriented shells, with no within-component T junction or self intersection among the tested exact facet pairs. It found eight **cross-component intersection segments**, four each for selectors 15/16 and 17/18, with exact points in both bounded interiors. These crossings predate seam repair. The per-component AABB proof survives overlaps, but the priority partition cannot be called 18 separate physical coils without mapping evidence. The [independent exact first-event oracle](P00_STEP3_REPORT_20261001.md) found three touching-cube order-dependent component/normal counterexamples in a combined-component kernel test, while its 738 sampled original-facet P00 queries had no hard mismatch. A [repeat on the derived transport payload](P00_STEP3_DERIVED_EVIDENCE_20261001_01/P00_STEP3_RECEIPT_20261001.json) again had no hard mismatch in 738 P00 queries (495 match, 111 expected unresolved, 132 conservative unresolved); the same three analytic combined-cube failures remained. Native `SurfaceFacetSet` instances filter to one component and OpenMC combines them through the region expression, so the combined-kernel cube result alone does not demonstrate a wrong native trajectory. A subsequent [native separate-surface assembly audit](P00_ASSEMBLY_REPORT_20261001.md) **did** find shared pre-AABB continuation aborts, a skipped representable tiny gap with wrong owner/current, and actual-P00 intersection-loop owner/contact discrepancies. Its 680 deterministic rows are identical between matched baseline/current builds. Continuous CAD and all-ray numerical behavior remain open. Source births in the flat-ring proxy differ from P00, so its event counts and transport seconds cannot be assigned entirely to geometry overhead.

## Mathematical options to investigate

| Candidate | Potential value | Proof/measurement gate |
|---|---|---|
| Count and localize classification, distance, normal and crossing work | Locates enough removable work to matter | Instrumented counters and time by call path, reported separately from uninstrumented transport |
| Near-first exact BVH traversal for distance | May prune candidate triangles sooner | Preserve earliest ambiguous event, ties and priority ownership; compare full hit/normal manifests before native transport |
| A top-level component BVH | May avoid repeated queries across 18 selectors | Bound must be conservative in the actual coordinate frame; prove Boolean priority cannot discard a nearer valid owner |
| Robust ray-triangle predicate with exact/adaptive fallback | Could reduce false seam ambiguity | Preserve all existing fail-closed cases and derive error bounds, including degenerate facets; benchmark fallback rate |
| Generalized winding/solid-angle classification | Alternative to three parity rays | Approximate fast winding values alone cannot certify exact side near boundaries; requires an error interval and fallback |
| Deterministic Boltzmann MOC or discrete ordinates | May suit a different multigroup calculation | Changes solver, source/energy approximation and tally contracts; evaluate as a separate study, not an equal-result kernel optimization |

The immediate target is demanding: with the two new seeds, candidate transport is 24.648/25.985 s and rings 10.408/9.962 s. Holding the ring times fixed, the custom code must save about **47% and 52%** of its current total transport time to reach 1.25×. A local call optimization cannot meet this alone unless the measured affected call path occupies at least that share.

"Deterministic geometry" here means repeatable point/ray queries inside Monte Carlo transport. A deterministic Boltzmann solver such as multigroup discrete ordinates changes the energy/angle/space approximations and cannot reproduce seed-matched Monte Carlo raw sums and sum squares. It would need a separate time-to-accuracy study for the same physical responses. OpenMC's [random-ray method](https://docs.openmc.org/en/stable/methods/random_ray.html) is stochastic and multigroup; the [user guide](https://docs.openmc.org/en/stable/usersguide/random_ray.html) limits its supported tally scores, which excludes this P00 run's `damage-energy` score. Deterministic-adjoint-assisted variance reduction can be a separate study of estimator efficiency, but cannot establish this kernel's equivalence.
