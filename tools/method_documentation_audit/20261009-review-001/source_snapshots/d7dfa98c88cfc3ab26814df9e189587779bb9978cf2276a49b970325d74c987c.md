## Additional documented experiments

Adds an append-only method ledger, immutable input/source/result snapshots, and local synthetic trials of the supplied Fourier-torus and localized spline-join proposals. The first two supplied texts are identical; both provenance paths are recorded.

### Results
- Fourier-guided torus patches: sampled deviation falls from 8.927 mm to 1.185 mm with refinement, but adjacent patch gaps remain (14.326 mm to 1.932 mm). This is an approximate atlas, not a closed solid.
- Constant-weight circular-torus polynomial compilation remains quartic. Global fit rank saturates at 14; adding basis tori leaves measured error. No physical fidelity threshold was evaluated.
- Torus plateaus with cubic-Hermite join strips produce a closed C1 synthetic radial-profile surface in a shared BVH. The periodic all-spline comparator is more accurate for this target. Narrow strips can concentrate normal error; candidate work is counted separately from selected hits.
