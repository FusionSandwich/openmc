The Sc-48 question concerns UWNR Ti activation gamma analysis, separate from P00 transport. The original Quantum reports and activities remain source records; no corrected activity or physical C/E is adopted here. The existing UWNR owner's [bounded handoff](C:/Users/joshu/Documents/UWNR_work/rafm-publication/studies/uwnr_rafm/paper3_m1_mcnp/sc48_diagnosis_20261001/SC48_HANDOFF_2026-10-01.md) contains the source identities and earlier quantitative reconstruction.

A bounded read-only review of hash-matched native files found two discriminating clues:

1. All three Ti ANS files contain identical float32 efficiency coefficients that match the recovered April 30 candidate CSV at printed precision. The CSV labels `DI=1.39`; the original Quantum report prints `Detector Thickness (DI)=6.450 cm`, and the native file has float32 `6.45` at byte 640. Native byte 520 contains `1.39`. Coefficient agreement alone therefore does not prove the candidate CSV was the complete active calibration. The historical type-2 efficiency equation and field mapping are needed.
2. Native float32 energy coefficients `[-1.6943864, 0.49962062, 6.709742e-8]`, inverse-energy endpoint flooring, and inclusive channel summation reproduce all seven stored long-count gross ROI counts. The four Sc-48 ROIs are 175.4 keV channels 350–357/gross 59634, 983.5 channels 1965–1976/gross 21736, 1037.5 channels 2073–2085/gross 21240, and 1312.1 channels 2621–2636/gross 16545. This narrows gross-count extraction, not net background or activity processing.

The original report prints `RAD INT=1.00` for the 983.5 and 1312.1 keV lines while the 1037.5 line prints `97.60`. The [evaluated Ti-48 decay data](https://www.nndc.bnl.gov/ensnds/48/Ti/beta_decay.pdf) place the former near unit photon yield; the [historical Quantum manual](https://ludlums.com/images/product_manuals/QTMmanual.pdf) describes per-100-decay intensities, `USE` flags and `SUMM CORR`. This discrepancy is real source-QC evidence, but a 1% library probability, a factor-100 summing correction, and an efficiency scaled by 1/100 can be algebraically degenerate in one line's activity. Same-line decay ratios cannot separate constant multiplicative factors.

The [completed focused Pro followup](https://chatgpt.com/c/6abdb8a5-b920-83ea-86fd-287650eed25e) checked the historical manual's field layout: `EfCal(0 To 8)` occupies bytes 504–539, and `DetThickness` occupies byte 640. Thus native `1.39` at byte 520 is the fifth efficiency coefficient, while `6.45` at byte 640 is the separate thickness field. This removes the apparent same-field contradiction; it does not identify the type-2 equation or the active function. The manual also labels byte 540 `EfCalUnc`, without defining its usable physical uncertainty semantics. It describes a 1536-byte header but lists a tail extending through byte 1545, so the observed 1548 channel offset should be validated empirically rather than explained by a blanket byte shift. No version-specific Sc-48 vendor notice or documented type-2 interpretation was verified.

A parallel Sol read-only audit found the original Ti reports each state **“Library efficiencies were ignored.”** Root independently verified this at line 58 of all three original text reports and verified the first native ANS bytes. All three native files have zero bytes at 1292 immediately before the anchored `GammaLib.mdb` string at 1294, consistent with a false `UseLibEff` field in the locally anchored layout. Native bytes 496/498 decode to `EfCalFlag=-1` and `EfCalType=2`; byte 520 is `EfCal[4]=1.3899999857`, while byte 640 is the separate thickness `6.4499998093`. This narrows the hypothesis that a GammaLib-stored **efficiency** controlled the activity. It does not imply nuclear emission probabilities or summing corrections were ignored, nor does it demonstrate the executable's runtime branch. A searched December archive contained duplicate Ti reports/native spectra and the candidate efficiency CSV, but no GammaLib database, deployed executable, settings file or numerical calculation trace. The mechanism therefore remains unseparated.

The smallest decisive recovery is the deployed executable/version, the full enabled Sc-48 GammaLib rows with probability/`USE`/`SUMM CORR`, the active efficiency type-2 fields and geometry/attenuation options, and saved ROI/background/clock metadata. A controlled replay should vary one field at a time against preserved native gross/net counts and original reports.
