## What worked somewhat

**Local geometry and analytic specialization.** The original redesign moved broad searches into local periodic patches and swept spans with BVHs. Shared traversal across 48 coils is the strongest preserved set-level result. Exact circular dispatch is competitive with built-in geometry when admission is genuinely analytic. Both are worth preserving. Later wrong-first-root witnesses prevent restoring the old general coil solver unchanged. [S05, S07]

**Restricted correctness and internal acceleration.** The exact-control constant-metric offset representation resolved the frozen 160 scalar queries: 69 hits, 91 misses, zero scalar unresolved/wrong results under the retained independent certificates. Bernstein prefix acceleration reduced summed distance time from 3.50743 to 0.86565 s, a factor of 4.052; child wall improved by 2.605. An immutable interval cache also improved its own diagnostic workload. Nine normal/projection gaps remained, and imported-coil scalar rows were misses. These results do not qualify varying-frame rectangular packs or nearly native transport. [S13, S14, S15, S16]

**P00 priority components and distant-point classification.** Priority/component CSG took 0.37243 s versus 0.72726 s for a common-material union on a 360-history diagnostic. The conservative component-AABB classification exclusion avoids unnecessary parity rays. A first one-ULP proposal was rejected and replaced with a coincidence margin. Later derived-payload sampled classifications agree in all 1,944 point/component pairs. These are finite represented-mesh results, with physical fidelity, boundary and ownership gates still open. [S04, S09, S11]

**Integration and evidence infrastructure.** Native proxy tally/source tests, a spherical-mesh equator fix, transformed MeshFilter identity/HDF5 repair, hash-role validation and worker-failure propagation were useful progress. Exact dyadic plane arithmetic passed 5,520 differential rows. The offline counter checker rejects 65 negative controls. These are specific capabilities, not acceptance of arbitrary transport. [S08, S17, S22, S25, S26]

## What failed and what remains untested

The detailed ledger names individual attempts, mechanisms, failure witnesses and revisit conditions. Its main distinctions are:

- **Wrong or incomplete roots:** legacy Newton/sign scans, sampled Bernstein fallback, torus-arc Candidate B, Candidate D and point-pruned Bézier atlas attempts do not supply an admitted general solver. More seeds, tolerance changes or candidate-only speed do not repair missing prefix/even-root coverage. [S08, S12, S13]
- **Correct restricted work that remains too expensive:** global offset certification, repeated minima and ownership proofs. In one native diagnostic, exclusive offset-service cost was 67.19% certified minimum and 20.18% projection proof; these percentages do not describe all transport time. [S16]
- **Rejected shortcut:** a plasma direct analytic evaluate/normal replacement compiled but failed `PROBE_FAIL: sense changed`. Its exact failing point/direction was not captured. A cache-only alternative remained unbuilt. [S18]
- **Invalid success comparison:** inflated PCA ring envelopes made a 7.836/8.652 s comparison appear to pass, but their dimensions and occupancy did not represent the fixed-section proxy. The corrected pair failed. Near-circle old runs around 0.23 s also predict analytic specialization; they do not prove generic BVH speed. [S05]
- **Proposals without native acceptance:** ruled cubic/rational faces, offline ownership certificates, a canonical swept alternative, conservative bound hierarchies and exact native plane event repair. Keep reported cloud prototype checks separate from imported, compiled local evidence. [S08, S13, S19]
- **Unavailable comparison:** historical ordinary DAGMC is not Embree. A retained Double Down binary lacks its named runnable dependency stack in the reviewed environment; current experimental runtime had DAGMC disabled. A same-target verified Embree pack/plasma timing is NOT_RUN. This is not proof that Embree is slow. [S05, S23]

## The latest correctness blocker

The October 1 native assembly audit changes the next step. Separate OpenMC facet surfaces and actual Region/Particle/scoring paths produced 680 identical baseline/current deterministic rows, while both retained shared defects. Analytic touching components can continue into an abort. Derived P00 intersection-loop probes produced eight first-event discrepancies and eleven continuation aborts among sixteen rays per library. Exact certificates retain strictly positive separations. These void-model witnesses do not establish that a saved production history exercised the same failure. They do prevent broad navigation/scoring acceptance. [S11, S24]

A focused **built-in-plane** control isolates at least one failure beyond the custom facet adapter. A reverse ray exits B at x = 1 + 2^-48. The correct itinerary is B, then background for 2^-48 cm, then A. Native selection found the correct first plane but successor lookup immediately assigned A, skipping the positive background interval and its local score. The trace points to `Surface::sense()` treating a different nearby plane as coincident under `FP_COINCIDENT = 1e-12`. Other token orders also raise a separate distance-reduction issue. The baseline plane client timed out before rows, so this is one decisive current-library counterexample rather than a completed matched plane matrix. [S25]

True ties and distinct close contacts require different handling. A smaller global tolerance or numerical push does not prove correct ownership and interval scoring. The proposed exceptional-event path uses exact contact groups, strict one-sided successor membership and preserved positive intervals. It begins with a deliberately narrow one-level, void, unit-axis-plane subset. Extension to facets, non-unit rays, materials, transforms and other features is separate work. [S19, S22]

**Newest implementation state:** exact dyadic primitives passed standalone tests. A later source checkpoint wires the proposed opt-in native functional unit, but CMake configure returned 124 at its 60-second cap. No target compiled, no native rows ran, and no new library/client was produced. The known acceptance-fixture issue injects opposite-extreme plane offsets and fails to restore them before a second query. The logs also retain a WSL Git path problem and a failed HDF5 probe; neither establishes the timeout cause or a missing dependency. Preserve these failures. [S27, S28]

## Questions the independent review should resolve

1. Which methods remain worth developing after enforcing target fidelity, complete first-event/ownership semantics and elapsed transport cost? Challenge the current preference for P00 facets for packs and checked periodic patches for plasma.
2. What does each measured acceleration actually isolate? Identify compiler confounding, changed solids, occupancy, collision/tally workloads, short denominators, and unchecked failures.
3. Does the opt-in plane repair preserve contact order, complete equal-time groups, positive intervals, cell itinerary, scores, lifecycle and ABI under its stated subset? Inspect the uncompiled code and known fixture defect before proposing a new build.
4. Which missing artifacts would change the conclusion? Distinguish physical pack CAD/frame/owner metadata from implementation evidence and unavailable cloud prototypes.
5. Choose one bounded next action with a falsifiable acceptance criterion. The evidence currently favors diagnosing/reviewing native event repair before another P00 speed campaign, and measuring complete plasma queries before a wholesale solver rewrite. Treat this ordering as a recommendation to challenge, not a premise to accept.

No evidence here proves mathematical impossibility or that a new general theorem is necessary. The observed obstacles are specific representation, completeness, navigation, workload and implementation problems. [S04, S08, S11]
