## Plasma CSG selection

Continue the **older periodic local patch/BVH implementation** for nonaxisymmetric plasma, and preserve the **exact native torus specialization** for analytic torus inputs. These are separate paths, with their own correctness and timing gates.

For actual WISTELL plasma, the five-observation 10,000-history record gives transport medians **0.36314 s native patch/BVH** and **0.45281 s fine DAGMC**. Initialization and external wall times are absent. The recorded leakage fractions also differ, 0.9955 versus 1.0; the historical report marks accuracy closure incomplete. This is the strongest preserved plasma performance starting point inspected, not an accepted full-time/physics winner.

The 21-observation exact-torus study supports parity with native OpenMC but its compact record retains rates rather than elapsed timers. Do not compute a full-time result by reciprocating those rates. The later Astra exact-torus proxy's 62.08% transport rate is not evidence that the nonaxisymmetric plasma path is better than the preserved implementation.

Root source inspection found a concrete regression in adapter optimization: the composite periodic adapter cached exact torus parameters and root options and directly called `openmc::torus_distance`, with algebraic classification/normal. The current Astra adapter lacks that direct branch and constructs root options per distance call. Restoring the reviewed analytic specialization is a concrete next action. Preserve current exact coefficient detection, forced-general/reference selectors, and generic typed failure checks; do not copy the older `found ? distance : INFTY` behavior over current unresolved handling.
