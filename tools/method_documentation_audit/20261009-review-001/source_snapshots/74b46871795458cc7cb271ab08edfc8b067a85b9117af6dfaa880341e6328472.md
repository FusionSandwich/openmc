localization is prepared but **not executed**, pending root review.

### Touching cubes: downstream behavior is decisive

For A=[0,1]^3, B=[1,2]x[0,1]^2 and ascending ownership, the reverse source
`(1.75,.31,.42), u=(-1,0,0)` belongs to cell 2009. Original token order accepts
distance .75 on signed surface -1108 and enters cell 2008. It then advances
1 cm through A and reaches the complement. The first cell flux is .75 in
2009 and the next cumulative flux includes 1 in 2008. This valid itinerary
is not a failure merely because the selected contact belongs to A.

Reverse the equivalent cell tokens: the first step remains .75 and the owner
remains 2008, but selected surface becomes +1109. The next nearest-boundary
query explicitly aborts on facet surface 1108 at the coincident origin. The
forward touching ray also correctly enters B at its first event, then aborts
on B's unhinted coincident surface. The combined-kernel counterexample alone
did not predict which native token order was harmful; the native continuation
receipt establishes this limitation.

### A representable gap is lost

Set B's left edge to `1 + 2^-48`. From
`(1.7500000000000036,.31,.42), u=(-1,0,0)`, native surfaces return:

| Surface | Native returned distance |
|---|---:|
| 1109, exit from B | 0.75 |
| 1108, later entry into A | 0.7500000000000036 |

The exact itinerary is **2009 -> 2000 -> 2008**, with a positive void segment
`1/281474976710656` cm. Original token order retains the farther -1108 contact
in both Region::distance and distance_complex and directly enters 2008. It
scores `0.7500000000000036` cm to 2009, no gap length to 2000, and current -1
to surface 1108 instead of first exiting surface 1109. This is a measured
step/owner/score defect, not a tolerance-only selected-ID observation.

Reversed tokens choose +1109 at .75 but still assign successor 2008, showing
that fixing the distance selection alone is insufficient. In the tiny-overlap
reversed-token control the farther B exit can likewise replace the earlier
A entry even when the eventual cell owner is correct. The exact slab review
detects this below the generic 2e-9 distance-comparison tolerance.

FP_PRECISION is 1e-14 and FP_COINCIDENT is 1e-12 in the pinned native source.
Region's relative-minimum test can explain the farther candidate retained
in the original-order gap example. Facet side/normal coincidence and subsequent
origin handling also need localization before a production repair is chosen.
The 2^-30 gap controls preserve the sampled itinerary; 2^-40 controls can
reach the correct first successor then abort at the subsequent near contact.

### Actual-derived P00

All eight rays from the two exact overlap witnesses and all 36 component face
entry/exit controls match at the first event. Of the sixteen +/-x rays aimed
through the eight saved intersection segment midpoints, eight match and eight
have first-event ownership/contact discrepancies. Eleven of the sixteen rays
subsequently abort on an unresolved facet nearest-boundary query.

The rounded midpoint rays do **not** establish mathematical exact ties.
The [expanded exact interval certificates](p00-assembly-20261001-05/derived/exact-failure-intervals.json)
show strictly positive rational contact separations for all eight first-event
failures, ranging from approximately **6.398e-16 to 1.465e-14 cm**. They retain
first/next exact contact fractions, intermediate rational points, and exact
parity certificates for components 15–18. Seven failures select a first contact
consistent with the oracle but assign the wrong outgoing owner. One also
selects the later conflicting component contact. These are results on the
represented binary64 rays, not on an ideal unrounded segment-center ray.

Native tally vectors follow the actual chosen predecessor and surface; they
are preserved in full. A wrong outgoing owner changes the next scoring segment
or leads to an explicit continuation abort, so the first-event MATCH census
alone cannot close the publication geometry gate.

## Focused review findings

**Blocking:** native sampled event itineraries are not uniformly accepted;
tiny positive intervals can be lost/misattributed and touching/near contacts
can abort on continuation. Treat them as native acceptance failures or explicit
limitations; do not add a cross-component blanket abort solely from the
standalone cube result.

**Verified:** actual separate selectors and native Boolean dispatch were used;
matched library raw-row equality holds; first-event resolved score-bin controls
match; adversarial collapsed-gap/step/bin controls reject corruption; shared
source/evidence and resource limits were preserved. No new dependencies.

**Limits:** finite rays only; no full stochastic histories, nuclear scores,
continuous CAD accuracy, physical-coil identity, complete periodic wrapping,
performance qualification, or proof for all magnitudes/rays. Built-in planes
and corrective layer localization remain unexecuted. No publication acceptance
or blanket scientific conclusion is warranted from these tests.
