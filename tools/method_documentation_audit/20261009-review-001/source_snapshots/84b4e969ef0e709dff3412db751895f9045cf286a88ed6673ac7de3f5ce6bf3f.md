Read `../REMAINING_WORK_DISPATCH.md`, `../CLOUD_METHOD_HANDOFF.md` and the
numerical-review section of `../REPAIR_07_CHECKPOINT.md`. Follow their resource,
branch, model-truth and evidence rules. This is an independent reviewer lane,
not another solver implementation. Do not rerun the ongoing failed transport
case or change production kernels. Own only a new review document and focused
tests under `dev/stellarcsg/experiments/certification_audit/`.

Audit the whole-span geometry admission requirements, binary64 evaluation
enclosures, origin-contact/tangent conventions and nearest-root contract.
Separate ideal spline mathematics from actual evaluated frame, Horner and
trigonometric operations. Cover center-derivative regularity and independence
of the supplied normal, zero-proxy candidates, unresolved intervals and solved
prefixes. Explain which assumptions the retained 128 BLOCKED geometries violate
without changing their reasons or declaring them physically invalid.

t must be resolved or excluded. Establish which existing tests verify examples
and which conclusions require an actual argument. Small Newton residuals,
sign changes, samples and finite precision bounds lacking rounding analysis
are not certificates. Make no blanket correctness declaration from the corpus.

Develop compact analytic/adversarial tests independent of the WISTELL-D
assembly, including contact, even roots, near multiplicity, transforms and
direction scaling. Cross-check against exact or analytic references where
domains overlap. Keep reference-root conventions explicit. Do not author the
same optimization that you independently approve; unreviewed future patches
remain pending. Return actionable file/line findings, minimal counterexamples,
admission recommendations, evidence hashes and runnable tests. Preserve negative
results and separate supported, blocked, unresolved and wrong outcomes.
