October 1, 2026. This audit concerns the **transported, seam-repaired 3,348-facet payload with 18 selectors**, in its retained centimetre query frame. It supports geometric component-AABB exclusion for that finite represented geometry. It also finds two genuine component-overlap pairs that prevent interpreting the selectors as disjoint physical-coil owners.

## Exact claims tested

The executable [audit](P00_STEP2_CERTIFICATE_20261001.py), [machine receipt](P00_STEP2_CERTIFICATE_20261001.json), and [terminal output](P00_STEP2_CERTIFICATE_20261001.stdout.txt) bind the inputs and source hashes. Python 3.12.10, NumPy 2.5.1 and h5py 3.16.0 were already installed. No production source, dependency environment, build, transport worker or remote system was changed.

Every binary64 vertex was interpreted as its **exact dyadic rational** using Python `Fraction`. All coordinates are finite. Every triangle has exact nonzero area and three distinct vertices; no duplicated triangle occurs within a component. For each selector 8–25:

- Every edge has exactly two incident facets in opposite directions.
- Every vertex link is one connected cycle, ruling out pinched-vertex topology.
- There is one connected shell, with strictly positive exact signed volume and consistent orientation.
- Exhaustive triangle pairs, after coordinate-AABB exclusion, intersect only in their shared combinatorial vertex or edge. All 25,431 remaining pair predicates use exact rational arithmetic. No self intersection, T junction, nonadjacent contact, coplanar overlap or duplicate facet was found.
- Every stored vertex lies in the box reconstructed by the kernel's triangle-extrema, one-ULP outward padding and box union. The full vertex arrays and component bounds are retained in the JSON receipt.

Together these checks establish an embedded closed orientable shell with outward orientation for every component of this **stored mesh**. Ten shells have Euler characteristic 2 and eight have Euler characteristic 0. Their bounded interiors may be nonconvex; the AABB argument does not require convexity. The detector also rejects deliberate noncoplanar crossing, coplanar overlap, shared-edge overlap and T-junction controls while accepting valid shared vertices/edges and disjoint triangles.

Let B be the component's vertex-extrema box. Every affine triangle is in B because its points are convex combinations of its vertices. A point outside B can be connected to infinity without crossing the component boundary; therefore it is exterior to its bounded fill. The kernel uses a still larger box: one outward ULP followed by `1e-8 * max(1, abs(all six bounds))`. The observed margins range from approximately 9.10e-6 to 1.56e-5 cm. Thus **the exact geometric exterior-exclusion premise holds on the transported payload itself**. The margin is a compatibility precaution; this audit does not convert it into a universal floating-point error theorem.

The retained native geometry has 18 selectors in one universe, no cell or surface translation/rotation, and centimetre payload coordinates. The adapter copies query x/y/z directly and filters the payload by the selector before constructing its BVH. This is the current quarter-instance query frame. Rotated unfolded copies need corresponding rotated bounds; continuous CAD patches need their own bounds.

## Remaining proof obligations

The geometric certificate does not prove legacy parity/BVH/adapter operational equivalence for arbitrary floating-point queries. A geometric exterior may previously have generated `nullopt` from a seam ray; the AABB fast path can make that state definite exterior. `SurfaceFacetSet::evaluate()` treats a disengaged optional with a usable normal as zero, and otherwise throws. Preserving that behavior requires examining coincidence/normal acceptance states, rather than proving membership alone.

`normal_at()` uses a plane tolerance and barycentric lower bounds of -1e-12, with a rounded Gram determinant. This mesh's smallest diagnostic squared-sine Gram ratio is approximately 0.001338008, and its smallest double area is approximately 3.846167 cm², so no extremely thin triangle was observed. Those diagnostic numbers are not interval bounds for all intermediate C++ operations, all query magnitudes or all arithmetic formats. A proof that every legacy coincidence acceptance point lies within the expanded box remains open. The six-face 1e-13 cm regression and retained transport comparisons support only their tested states.

Arbitrary first-boundary distance, ambiguity ordering, tie ownership, cap/tangent crossing, periodic physical-coil ownership, continuous CAD/frame/section fidelity, and publication-level per-physical-coil tally interpretation remain open. The finite mesh is suitable for a **conditional represented-solid AABB membership claim**. Full physical winding-pack qualification remains unavailable.

An open cube missing one face is a counterexample to using a box as an operational parity-soup shortcut without a bounded-fill contract: a ray from beyond the missing face can cross the opposite face once. A closed edge-count mesh with a pinched vertex defeats edge counts alone. The exact vertex-link and embeddedness checks here address those finite topology defects; they do not solve the remaining numerical and physical obligations.
