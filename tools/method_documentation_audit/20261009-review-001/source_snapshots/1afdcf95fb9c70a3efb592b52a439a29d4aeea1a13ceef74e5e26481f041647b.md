## Change and correctness boundary

`CompiledFacetSurfaceSet::contains` now returns exterior when the point is farther than a small coincidence margin outside the component facet AABB. This avoids three BVH parity rays for distant components. The margin is `1e-8 * max(1, abs(each box coordinate))` in the existing centimetre coordinates; queries within it retain the old parity and normal path. This is a conservative geometric exclusion for a closed facet solid, subject to the existing facet model and floating-point assumptions. `distance()` and boundary normals are unchanged.

The first one-ULP padding proposal failed independent Sol review: a point `1e-13 cm` outside a cube face changed from unresolved parity with a usable normal to definite exterior. That proposal was replaced with the coincidence margin and a regression test. A distant exterior parity ray through a seam can now resolve directly as exterior; this is an intentional operational change. Independent review found no demonstrated unsafe ownership change after repair. Exhaustive floating-point proof for degenerate triangles, all seams, and all OpenMC adapter states has **not** been established. The local unit test passed.

The standalone fixture has 3,348 facets, 18 components and 108 query points. Across 100 sweeps, 194,400 classifications agreed in aggregate: 5,400 inside, 189,000 outside and zero unresolved for both versions. Of 1,944 point/component pairs, 1,788 are outside the raw component bounds. Two baseline loop times were 0.361729 and 0.351010 s; candidate times were 0.145305 and 0.155000 s. This measures the classification loop on this fixture, **not** native transport or arbitrary query distributions. The [probe source](../experiments/p00_classification_probe_20260930.cpp) and [runner](../experiments/p00_classification_20260930.sh) describe the frozen test; local output is in `p00-classification-20260930-03/`.
