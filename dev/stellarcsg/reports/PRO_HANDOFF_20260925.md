## What was tried and did not qualify

- The recovered-old fast swept solver gives wrong nearest roots for strict bank IDs **a03, a06, a08, a15** against the stronger exact-reference lane. The final-iterate residual recheck built and passed standalone tests but did not repair those four failures; its seven-repeat candidate was slower than old on that bank. See the detailed receipt sections *Frozen-bank replay* and *Final-iterate residual experiment*. Do not promote either kernel as correct.
- Floating-frame and endpoint-sphere audit witnesses reproduced under GCC and Clang; they are focused counterexamples, not a full WISTELL-D query proof. The compiled-centerline bound audits address only part of this failure space. The next interrupted experiment was to dump **actual compiled BVH nodes** and audit parent/child enclosures and traversal. No new helper or long-running test was started at this checkpoint.
- A 10 × 8 cm diagnostic ellipse is substantially smaller than P00's nominal 30 × 30 cm rectangular coil section. Two ellipse/frame candidates differ by up to 1.84 cm in sampled section support. The local ParaStell edge controls describe nominal pre-CAD corners but do not bind the exact accepted CAD revision. The optimization repository's 30 cm *continuous magnet layer* is a separate half-period approximation, not a per-coil winding-pack definition.
- Initial periodic facet caps, XML selectors, and standalone native particle setup had defects that were corrected and rerun. The final named receipts above supersede failed staging attempts. The component intersections remain real in the accepted facets; a diagnostic priority partition cannot justify distinct per-coil materials or tallies.
- `source_mesh.h5m` and `strengths.npy` in the optimization repository are real source-data inputs, but have not passed the independent fixed-wall and moving-plasma clearance needed for a one-period source. Their SHA-256 values are `65264e15669d09c43f107c3b43c2af24ffbd15173e3bbd0e990b527bfa0b5322` and `0ed18ab58bcc1e9884bf1b5c8bf19a7b7558ce7afe1869f1a2b01710148af6df`.
