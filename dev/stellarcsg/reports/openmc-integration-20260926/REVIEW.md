Ordinary MeshSource is a suitable tested interface for the intended eventual VMEC adapter. Resampling can therefore change the spatial profile and energy correlations; it cannot rescue an asserted original PDF or establish its physical source rate. A whole-mesh containment certificate against the actual CSG model is required before physical use.
