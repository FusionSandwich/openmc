CALL GRAPH
OpenMC tracking
  -> SurfaceSweptSpline::distance
  -> CompiledSweptSplineSurface::distance_reference
  -> find_nearest_root_reference over the complete coil bounding-box ray interval
  -> evaluate for every root-function sample
  -> local_coordinates
  -> squared_distance over 4*sample_count centerline samples
  -> 48 golden-section iterations around the best global sample
  -> frame, which samples 11 periodic spline fields

The numerical ray derivative is a centered finite difference. Each derivative
sample therefore calls evaluate twice, and each evaluate repeats the complete
centerline scan and golden-section refinement. OpenMC requests 48 initial ray
subdivisions and up to 6 global refinement levels. The root solver additionally
searches stationary points and bisects detected brackets.

STATIC WORK ACCOUNTING
For N centerline samples, one generic evaluate performs 4N squared-distance
checks plus approximately 100 additional squared-distance/frame evaluations
from golden-section setup and its 48 iterations. Each frame evaluates center x,
y,z; normal x,y,z; binormal x,y,z; and two radii. A root sample plus its numeric
derivative requires three complete evaluate calls. This work is repeated for
every globally sampled t value before any local hit is accepted.

DIAGNOSIS
CONFIRMED: the generic swept production path nests a global ray search around a
global closest-centerline search. This directly explains the roughly 4.2 seconds
of transport per history and the 841x matched-mesh gap. The replacement needs a
two-level coil/span BVH and a local (span,cross-section) solve; the full
centerline and global ray scans cannot remain on the production path.
